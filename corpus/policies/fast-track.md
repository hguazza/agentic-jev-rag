# Fast-Track Recruitment

Fast-track is SwiftAI's service for clients who need a role filled quickly without lowering the bar.

## Service levels

- **Shortlist within 48 hours** of the client approving the job description.
- **Screening verdicts within 24 hours** of the shortlist.
- **First interviews within 5 working days** of the job being opened.
- The client gets a Recruitment report after each stage.

## What fast-track changes

- The Sourcer considers only candidates with valid consent who have told us they are open to new roles.
- Screening and first interviews may be held by the AI Recruiter, but every rejection and every offer recommendation is reviewed by a human recruiter before the candidate is told.
- Shortlists are capped at 8 candidates so screening stays fast.

## What fast-track never changes

The Fair Hiring Policy, the Data Protection and Consent Policy and the Interview Guide apply in full. Speed is never a reason to skip a compliance check.
