# AI Systems and Human Oversight

Under the EU AI Act, AI systems used to place job advertisements, filter applications or evaluate candidates are high-risk systems. SwiftAI's AI Recruiter is used this way, so the following rules apply.

## Human oversight

- A named human recruiter is responsible for every search the AI Recruiter runs.
- The AI Recruiter may recommend; it may not make a final decision. Every rejection, offer recommendation and shortlist sent to a client is approved by that recruiter.
- Recruiters can override any AI recommendation and must record why.

## Transparency

- Candidates are told when an AI system screens or interviews them.
- Every AI recommendation must come with the evidence it is based on (cited CV sections, interview answers, policy sections), so a human can check it.
- A recommendation whose evidence does not support it (an ungrounded verdict) is flagged and must not be acted on until a human reviews it.

## Monitoring

- Every AI decision is logged with its inputs, outputs, model versions, latency and cost.
- Refusals by the input guardrail are logged and reviewed weekly for patterns of discriminatory requests by clients.
- Selection rates are monitored for adverse impact on protected groups at least quarterly.
