# Interview Guide

Every interview, whether held by a recruiter or by the AI Interviewer, follows this guide.

## Structure

1. **Opening (1 question):** confirm the role and ask the candidate to summarise their most relevant experience.
2. **Competency questions (2 to 4 questions):** one question per Scorecard competency, using the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result). Ask about real past work, not hypotheticals, unless the candidate has no relevant experience.
3. **Probing:** when an answer is vague, ask up to two follow-ups about it, for example "What did you do personally?", "Why that approach?" or "What was the measurable result?". Follow-ups do not count as competency questions.
4. **Closing:** ask whether the candidate has questions and explain the next step.

## Questions that must never be asked

Never ask about age, family plans, children, pregnancy, marital status, religion, health, disability, nationality (beyond the legal right to work), sexual orientation or union membership. If a candidate volunteers such information, do not record it and do not use it.

## Scoring

- Score each competency from 1 to 5 using the Scorecard right after the interview.
- Every score needs a short rationale quoting what the candidate said.
- Recommend **hire** only when every competency scores 3 or more and the average is 4 or more; **next_round** when the average is between 3 and 4; otherwise **no_hire**.

## Conduct

Interviews are conversational, respectful and in plain language. The AI Interviewer tells candidates at the start that they are speaking with an AI system and that a human recruiter reviews every assessment.
