# Fair Hiring Policy

The agency selects candidates on job-related merit only. This policy applies to every recruiter, every client search and every automated agent, including the AI Recruiter.

## Protected characteristics

No search, filter, ranking, screening decision or interview question may select, exclude, rank or score a candidate based on any of these characteristics, or on a proxy for them:

- age or date of birth
- sex, gender, gender identity or gender expression
- marital or civil partnership status, pregnancy, maternity or paternity, and family or caring responsibilities
- race, colour, ethnic or national origin, nationality or citizenship (except a lawful right-to-work requirement)
- religion or belief
- disability or health condition
- sexual orientation
- trade union membership or political opinion

## Indirect discrimination

A criterion that looks neutral but disadvantages a protected group is also prohibited unless the client can show it is necessary for the job. Requests the AI Recruiter must refuse include:

- "digital natives only", "young and dynamic team", "recent graduates only" for a non-graduate role, or a maximum number of years of experience (age proxies)
- "no career gaps" or "no gaps in employment" (disadvantages parents, carers and people with health conditions)
- "native English speaker" when the job needs a level of English, not a nationality (use "professional working proficiency in English" instead)
- "must live within 5 km of the office" when hybrid work is offered (can act as an ethnic or social proxy)
- "culture fit" without a written, job-related definition
- photos, names or addresses used as ranking signals

## What recruiters and agents must do

1. Refuse a request that uses a protected characteristic or an obvious proxy, explain which part of this policy it breaks, and offer a job-related alternative.
2. Base every Screening verdict on the job's Scorecard competencies and cite the evidence for each score.
3. Never infer a protected characteristic from a CV (for example age from graduation year) and never record one in notes.
4. Escalate to the compliance lead any client who repeats a discriminatory request.
