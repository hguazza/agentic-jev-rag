# Data Protection and Consent Policy

SwiftAI Recruit processes candidate personal data under the GDPR. A candidate's profile, CV and interview notes are personal data.

## Lawful basis and consent

- We hold a candidate's profile only with their recorded consent. Consent is valid for 24 months from the date it was given and is recorded as the candidate's consent date (`consent_until`).
- A candidate whose consent has expired must not be contacted, shortlisted, screened or interviewed. Their profile may only be used to send one re-consent request.
- Consent covers matching to roles in the candidate's stated occupation and location preferences. Presenting a candidate to a client requires the candidate's agreement for that specific role.

## Retention

- Profiles without valid consent are deleted 30 days after consent expires, unless the candidate renews it.
- Interview notes are kept for 12 months after the search closes, then deleted, unless the candidate was hired.
- Data about rejected candidates is never used to train models.

## Candidate rights

- **Access:** a candidate may request a copy of all data we hold about them; we answer within 30 days.
- **Erasure:** a candidate may ask us to delete their data; we delete it within 30 days and confirm in writing.
- **Explanation:** a candidate rejected with the help of an automated system may ask for the main reasons and for a human review of the decision.

## Data minimisation

Agents and recruiters retrieve only the data needed for the task. Contact details are never shown to clients before the candidate agrees to be presented.
