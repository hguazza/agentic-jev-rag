# Scorecards

Each job is assessed on the competencies for its occupation, each scored from 1 (no evidence) to 5 (strong, repeated evidence at the required seniority).

## Software engineering

- **Technical depth:** command of the languages, frameworks and systems the job requires.
- **System design:** designs services that scale, fail safely and are easy to change.
- **Delivery:** ships working software to production and owns it afterwards.
- **Collaboration:** works well in code review, with product and across teams.

## Information technology

- **Technical operations:** runs and supports infrastructure, networks, systems and end-user technology reliably.
- **Problem solving:** diagnoses incidents and fixes root causes, not just symptoms.
- **Project delivery:** plans and delivers IT projects and rollouts on time.
- **Stakeholder service:** communicates clearly with users and business teams and manages vendors.

## Data science

- **Statistics and modelling:** chooses and validates models appropriately.
- **Data engineering:** builds reliable data pipelines and features.
- **Business impact:** turns analysis into decisions with measurable results.
- **Communication:** explains results to non-technical audiences.

## Human resources

- **Employment practice:** knows employment law, policies and HR processes.
- **Talent acquisition:** sources, assesses and closes candidates.
- **Employee relations:** handles sensitive cases fairly and confidentially.
- **HR systems and data:** uses HRIS and people data accurately.

## Sales

- **Pipeline generation:** finds and qualifies opportunities.
- **Closing:** negotiates and wins deals against targets.
- **Account management:** grows and retains customers.
- **Product knowledge:** explains the product's value in the customer's terms.

## Design

- **Visual and interaction design:** produces clear, usable interfaces.
- **User research:** grounds decisions in evidence about users.
- **Design systems and tools:** works fluently in modern tools and systems.
- **Collaboration:** works with engineering and product to ship.

## Finance

- **Accounting and reporting:** produces accurate statements and reconciliations.
- **Financial analysis and modelling:** builds forecasts and models that drive decisions.
- **Controls and compliance:** applies internal controls and regulation.
- **Business partnering:** advises budget holders clearly.

## AI engineering

- **LLM systems:** agents, tool calling, prompt chains and orchestration (LangGraph, LangChain or LlamaIndex).
- **Retrieval and evaluation:** RAG architecture, vector databases and deterministic evaluation harnesses.
- **Production engineering:** Python and FastAPI services that are low-latency, tested and observable.
- **Cloud delivery:** deploys, monitors and scales on AWS; ships customer-facing features fast.

## Rule for advancing

A Screener advances a candidate when no competency scores 1 and the average is 3 or more. A candidate who does not meet a hard requirement in the job (for example a mandatory licence or right to work) is rejected regardless of scores.
